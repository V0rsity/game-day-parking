This app is intended to help users in determining which days their courses may conflict with tow times for parking lots near Ross-Ade Stadium.

  • All upcoming home games are official as listed on ESPN.
  • All tow times are predictions based on past tow times and the game's start time, they are not official or updated in real-time.
  • Up-to-date tow times for lots near Ross-Ade are only posted on temporary signage at the lot entrances.
  • This app is for personal use and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Purdue University or Purdue Parking Operations.
Very Likely

The course occurs during or after 5:00 PM, typically the latest tow time for lots near Ross-Ade.

Likely

The course occurs within 2 hours of the game start time. 4:30 PM tow times are typical for 6:30 PM games.

Possible

The course occurs within 2.5 hours of the game start time. These early tow times could be possible for larger games.
